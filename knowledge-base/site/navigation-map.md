# Where is everything on Let Me Lens? (navigation map)

## Which pages can anyone open without an account?
- **Home page** (`letmelens.com`): click the **Let me Lens** logo at the top left of the home, sign-in
  and Privacy Notice pages. Top menu: **Exemple**, **Why Us**, **Sign in** (or **Open gallery** when
  signed in). Main button: **Start the journey** (or **Open gallery** when signed in).
- **A photographer's gallery** (`letmelens.com/<profile-link>`): opened from a link someone shared, from
  **Exemple** / **Example** on the home page, or from **Satea ALMALLOUHI** in any footer.
  Top menu: **Collections**, **Photos**, **Contact**.
- **A collection page**: click a collection card in the **Collections** row of a gallery.
- **A photo**: click any photo in a grid. It opens in a viewer window over the page; the **✕** at the
  top closes it.
- **Privacy Notice** (`letmelens.com/privacy`): **Privacy Notice** link in the home page footer, or
  **Privacy Notice** under the sign-up form.

## Where are the sign-up and sign-in pages?
- **Create account** page (`/signup`): **Start the journey** or **Sign up** on the home page, or
  **Create one** under the sign-in form.
- **Welcome back** page, the sign-in page (`/login`): **Sign in** at the top right of the home page, or
  **Sign in** under the sign-up form ("Already registered?").
- **Verify your email** page (`/verify-email`): opens automatically after **Create account**.
- **Forgot your password?** page (`/forgot-password`): **Forgot password?** link on the sign-in page.
- **Create a new password** page (`/reset-password`): opened only from the link in the password reset
  email.

## How do I get into My Studio, the area where I manage my portfolio?
My Studio is only for signed-in photographers.
- **On a computer**: open your gallery (after signing in you land on it, or click **Open gallery** on
  the home page), click **Profile** at the top right, then **My Studio**.
- **On a phone**: open your gallery, tap the menu icon (three lines) at the top right, then
  **Configuration**.
My Studio opens on **Profile overview**. The top bar shows **Manage** with your display name,
**View gallery** and **Sign out**. The address bar shows `letmelens.com/<profile-link>/manage/...`.

## What is in the My Studio menu?
The left menu of My Studio is titled **Configuration** (on a phone, open it with the three-line menu
icon in the top bar). Its groups and links are:
- **Photos**
  - **New photo**: the upload queue ("Queue new photos").
  - **Configure photos**: **Homepage photo order**, where you drag photos and open **Edit**.
- **Collection**
  - **New collection**: the form to create a collection.
  - **Configure collection**: the list of your collections and the editor of the selected one.
- **Profile**
  - **Profile overview**: the whole "Profile configuration" page with the **Save profile** button.
  - **About me**: **Display name** and **Bio**.
  - **Social media**: **Public email**, **LinkedIn username**, **Instagram username**.
  - **Colors**: **Primary color**.
  - **Carousel / Slides**: the "Manage Slides" page to choose and order the slides at the top of your
    profile.
- **Account**
  - **Statistics**: "Portfolio statistics" (visits).
  - **Account overview**: the "Account settings" page.
  - **Profile link**: change `letmelens.com/<profile-link>` (**Save link**).
  - **Password**: change your password (**Update password**).
  - **Delete account**: permanently delete the account.
The arrow button at the top of the menu collapses or expands it.

## How do I leave My Studio or sign out?
- **View gallery** in the My Studio top bar opens your public gallery.
- **Sign out** is in the My Studio top bar, and in the **Profile** menu of the gallery (**Sign out**
  under **My Studio**). On a phone, both are in the three-line menu.

## Where do I edit or delete one photo?
Open the photo while signed in, from your gallery or from **Configure photos → Edit**. The buttons
**Edit photo** and **Delete photo** appear next to the photo's title.
