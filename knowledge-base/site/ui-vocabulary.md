# Words used on Let Me Lens and what they mean (glossary)

## What is a collection (album) on Let Me Lens?
On Let Me Lens a **collection** is a named group of photos with an optional description, like an
album. The website mostly says "collection" (**Collections**, **New collection**,
**Configure collection**, **View collection**), but two buttons say "album": **Create album** and
**Delete album**. Both words mean the same thing. A photo can belong to several collections, and the
first photo of a collection is used as its cover.

## What are slides, the carousel or the hero on Let Me Lens?
The **slides** are the large photos that scroll automatically at the very top of a gallery page, every
7 seconds, with arrows to move by hand. Owners choose them in **My Studio → Carousel / Slides**
(the page is titled "Manage Slides"). If no slide is chosen, the first photos of the gallery are shown
instead.

## What is My Studio, Configuration or Manage on Let Me Lens?
These three names point to the same private area where a signed-in photographer manages the
portfolio. The desktop **Profile** menu calls it **My Studio**, the phone menu calls it
**Configuration**, the left menu inside it is titled **Configuration**, and its top bar says
**Manage**. People may also call it the dashboard, the settings, the back office or the admin panel.

## What is a profile link or slug on Let Me Lens?
The **profile link** is the last part of a gallery address: in `letmelens.com/satea-almallouhi` it is
`satea-almallouhi`. The field that changes it is labelled **Gallery link slug** in
**Account → Profile link**. A new account gets a link made from the first and last name.

## What is the gallery, the profile or the portfolio on Let Me Lens?
They all mean the public page of one photographer at `letmelens.com/<profile-link>`. It shows the
slides, a card with the display name, bio and social icons, the **Collections** row and the **Photos**
grid. "Profile" in the menus also refers to the settings of that page (**Profile overview**).

## What do Display name, Bio and Primary color mean?
- **Display name**: the name shown on the gallery, on the bio card and in page titles. Required.
- **Bio**: the short text shown under the display name at the top of the gallery.
- **Primary color**: the main color applied to the gallery, chosen with a color picker or typed as a
  hex code like `#fcfcfc`.

## What are Themes on a photo?
**Themes** are categories shown under **More details** in the photo viewer: Street & Society,
People & Emotion, Nature & Environment, Architecture & Spaces, Conceptual & Artistic,
Documentary & Social. They help choose the **Related photos**. There is currently no button for owners
to set themes, so most photos show "—".

## What are Related photos?
**Related photos** are suggestions shown under a photo opened from the main gallery. They come from
the same photographer and favour photos with the same themes, the same country or the same city.

## What is the queue or Pending uploads?
The **queue** is the list of photos waiting to be uploaded on the **New photo** screen. Files stay
there, under **Pending uploads**, until you click **Save photos**.
