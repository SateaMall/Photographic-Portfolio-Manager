# What is Let Me Lens and why use it?

## What is Let Me Lens?
Let Me Lens is a portfolio website made especially for photographers. A photographer signs up, uploads
photos, groups them into **Collections**, and gets a public gallery at
`letmelens.com/<profile-link>` to share with clients, friends or recruiters. The home page describes it
as "Portfolios made especially for photographers" and says a portfolio can be prepared in 5 minutes.
Let Me Lens was created by Satea AlMallouhi.

## Why choose Let Me Lens for a photography portfolio?
The Let Me Lens home page highlights four strengths, each shown as a section when you scroll down
(the **Why Us** menu link jumps to them):
- **Elegant & Engaging**: "A refined gallery experience that invites interaction". Photos are shown in
  a justified grid, open in a large viewer with full-screen zoom, and suggest related photos.
- **Cross-Device Experience**: "A seamless experience adapted on all screens". The gallery adapts to
  phones, tablets and computers, with a mobile menu on small screens.
- **Customizable**: "Designed to match your artistic identity". The owner picks the slides at the top
  of the profile, a **Primary color**, a display name, a bio and social links.
- **Analytics**: "See how your portfolio performs over time". **Statistics** in My Studio show visits
  today, in the last 30 days and all time, plus a monthly or yearly chart.

## What else makes Let Me Lens practical?
- **Free**: no plan, subscription or payment. Let Me Lens was made free to support photographers,
  especially emerging ones who would hesitate because of the cost of a portfolio website.
- **Fast to set up**: sign up with email or **Continue with Google**, upload several photos at once,
  and the year is filled in automatically from the photo's camera data when available.
- **Drag-and-drop ordering**: photos, collections and slides are reordered by dragging, and every
  order change saves automatically.
- **Your own link**: the profile link (`letmelens.com/your-name`) can be changed in
  **Account → Profile link**.
- **Found by search engines**: public galleries, collections and photos have proper page titles,
  descriptions and preview images, and are listed in the site map, so they can appear in search
  results and look good when the link is shared on social networks.
- **Respect for your data**: only essential sign-in cookies are used, and the account with all photos
  can be deleted at any time from **Account → Delete account**.

## What does a Let Me Lens gallery look like?
The best way to see one is the example gallery of the site's creator: click **Exemple** in the top
menu of the home page, or **Example** in the "Ready to share your work?" section at the bottom of the
home page. Both open `letmelens.com/satea-almallouhi`.
