# Error messages on Let Me Lens and how to fix them

## Sign-in errors and what they mean
Messages shown on the Let Me Lens **Welcome back** (sign-in) page:
- "Invalid email or password." – the email or password is wrong. Check for typos, or reset the password
  with **Forgot password?**. If you signed up with Google and never set a password, use
  **Continue with Google**.
- "Please verify your email to continue." – your email is not verified yet. Enter the 4-digit code on
  **Verify your email**, or click **Resend code**.
- "This email already belongs to an account that still needs email verification." – appears when using
  **Continue with Google** with an email whose Let Me Lens account is not verified. Verify it first with
  the code, then Google sign-in works.
- "This account is already linked to a different Google sign-in." – the account is linked to another
  Google account; sign in with that one, or with email and password.
- "Your Google account must have a verified email address." – verify the email of your Google account
  at Google, then try again.

## Sign-up and verification errors
Messages shown on the Let Me Lens **Create account** and **Verify your email** pages:
- "Enter a valid email and a password with at least 8 characters." – fix the email or use a longer
  password.
- "Passwords do not match." – **Password** and **Confirm password** must be identical.
- "Email already exists" – an account already uses this email: sign in instead, or reset the password.
- "Invalid or expired verification code" – the code is wrong or older than 15 minutes; click
  **Resend code**.
- "Please wait 45 seconds before requesting another verification code." (the time varies) – wait 60
  seconds between two codes.
- "Too many verification code requests for this email. Please try again in …" – at most 6 codes per
  hour; wait the time shown.

## Password reset errors
Messages shown on the Let Me Lens password reset pages:
- "Invalid or expired password reset link." – the link is older than 30 minutes or was already used;
  request a new one with **Forgot password?**.
- "This password reset link is missing a token." – open the link exactly as it is in the email (copy
  the whole address).
- "New password must be at least 8 characters." / "New password and confirmation must match." – fix
  the new password fields.
- "Too many reset link requests for this email. Please try again in …" – at most 6 links per hour.

## Upload errors
Messages shown on the Let Me Lens **New photo** screen:
- "Skipped unsupported or duplicate files: …" – only JPEG, PNG and WEBP are accepted, and the same file
  cannot be queued twice. Convert HEIC or RAW files to JPEG.
- "Skipped files over 15MB: …" or "Upload failed: payload too large. Maximum allowed size is 15MB." –
  reduce the file size (export at a smaller size or higher compression) and try again.
- "File is not a readable image" – the file is damaged or not really an image; export it again.
- "Capture year must be between 1800 and 2100" – fix the **Year** field or leave it empty.

## Profile and collection errors
Messages shown in Let Me Lens **My Studio**:
- "That profile link is already taken." – choose another **Gallery link slug**.
- "Profile slug must be at least 3 characters." / "Profile slug must be 80 characters or fewer." –
  adjust the length of the link.
- "Display name is required." – **Display name** cannot be empty.
- "Primary color must be a valid hex value." – type a color like `#1a1a1a`, or use the color square.
- "Album title is required." – give the collection a **Title**.
- "This page is only available for your own main profile." – you opened the My Studio address of
  another gallery; use **Profile → My Studio** to open your own.
- A red message with **Retry save** – an automatic save failed; click **Retry save** or reload the page.

## My gallery link opens the home page instead of my gallery
On Let Me Lens, an address that does not match any gallery redirects to the home page. Check the
spelling of the link. If you recently changed your link in **Account → Profile link**, the old address
no longer works: share the new one.
