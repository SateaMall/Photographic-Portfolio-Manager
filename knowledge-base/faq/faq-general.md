# Frequently asked questions about Let Me Lens

## How much does Let Me Lens cost?
Let Me Lens is **free**. There is no plan, subscription or payment page. Its creator, Satea AlMallouhi,
made it free on purpose: photographers often do not earn much from their art, and a free portfolio helps
emerging photographers show their work without worrying about the cost of a website.

## Can I make my gallery, a collection or a photo private?
No. On Let Me Lens, everything you upload and every collection you create is public on your gallery as
soon as it is saved. There is no private, hidden or draft mode, and no password-protected gallery.
Only upload what you are happy to show publicly; delete a photo or collection to remove it.

## Do visitors need an account to see my gallery?
No. Anyone with your link (`letmelens.com/<your-link>`) can open your Let Me Lens gallery, collections
and photos without signing up. An account is only needed to create and manage your own gallery.

## Can I have several galleries with one account?
No. A Let Me Lens account has one gallery with one profile link. Use **Collections** to separate
different kinds of work inside the same gallery.

## Is there a Let Me Lens mobile app?
There is no app to install. The Let Me Lens website adapts to phones and tablets: open
`letmelens.com` in your phone's browser. On small screens the menus are behind the three-line icon, and
My Studio is called **Configuration** in that menu.

## In which language is Let Me Lens available?
The Let Me Lens interface is in English. Photo titles, descriptions, bios and collection names can be
written in any language.

## Can visitors download the photos I upload?
Let Me Lens has no download button: visitors can view your photos, open them full screen and share
their link. The site does not publish terms of use about photo rights; for such questions write to
**support@letmelens.com**. The **Privacy Notice** explains how your data is processed.

## Is my sign-in email shown on my gallery?
No. Let Me Lens never shows the email you sign in with. Visitors only see the **Public email** you choose
to add in **My Studio → Profile → Social media**, and only if you fill it in.

## Can visitors comment, like or follow?
No. Let Me Lens has no comments, likes, followers or messages. Visitors contact a photographer through
the email, LinkedIn or Instagram links shown on the gallery.

## How do I contact Let Me Lens?
Write to **support@letmelens.com**, the address shown in the home page footer and in the Privacy Notice.
There is no contact form or phone number.
