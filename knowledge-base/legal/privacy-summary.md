# How does Let Me Lens use my personal data? (Privacy Notice summary)

This is a plain-language summary of the Let Me Lens **Privacy Notice** (last updated 16 May 2026). The
full text is at `letmelens.com/privacy` and is the reference.

## What personal data does Let Me Lens collect?
According to its Privacy Notice, Let Me Lens collects: account data (email, password, first and last
name), verification and sign-in session data, Google account details if you use
**Continue with Google** (identifier, email, names, email verification status), the profile and
portfolio content you choose to publish (display name, bio, public email, social links, collections,
photos), and the messages you send to support.

## Why does Let Me Lens use this data?
Let Me Lens uses the data to create and run your account and portfolio, verify your email and sign you
in, display what you chose to publish, keep the service secure and fix problems, and meet legal
obligations. The legal bases given are the contract (running your account), legitimate interests
(security, support) and legal obligations.

## Who can see or receive my data?
Public visitors see only what you publish on your gallery. Hosting, storage, email-delivery and
technical providers process data to run the service, Google receives data if you use Google sign-in,
and authorities only when the law requires it. Data may be transferred outside the EU/EEA through
Google or a technical provider, with that provider's safeguards.

## How long is my data kept?
Account and portfolio data are kept while the account is active. Verification codes are kept until
used or expired, and session data until you sign out or the session expires. When you delete your
account (**My Studio → Account → Delete account**), the account and its portfolio content are removed
from the service, unless keeping something is required for security or legal reasons.

## Does Let Me Lens use cookies?
Let Me Lens uses only essential session cookies, to keep you signed in and secure the service. The
Privacy Notice states that consent would be asked before any analytics or marketing cookies are added.

## What are my rights and how do I use them?
You can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data by
writing to **support@letmelens.com**. You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection
authority.
