# Let Me Lens at a glance

## What is Let Me Lens?
Let Me Lens (letmelens.com) is a website where photographers create an online photography portfolio
and share it with a single link. Its tagline is "Let us help you share what matters to you", and the
home page promises a portfolio ready in about 5 minutes. Each photographer gets a public gallery page
at `letmelens.com/<their-profile-link>` with slides at the top, a short bio, social links,
**Collections** (groups of photos) and a **Photos** grid.

## Is Let Me Lens free?
Yes. Let Me Lens is free, with no plans or payments. It was made free on purpose, to support
photographers, especially emerging ones who might hesitate because of the cost of a portfolio website.

## Who made Let Me Lens?
Let Me Lens was designed and built by **Satea AlMallouhi** (written "Satea ALMALLOUHI" in the site
footer), a full-stack software engineer and photographer from Homs, Syria, based in Montpellier,
France. Satea's own portfolio, at `letmelens.com/satea-almallouhi`, is the example gallery that the
**Exemple** and **Example** buttons open. Recruiters can reach Satea on LinkedIn
(linkedin.com/in/satea-almallouhi).

## Who uses Let Me Lens?
- **Visitors** browse portfolios without an account: open collections, view photos full screen,
  see related photos, and contact the photographer through the links the photographer published.
- **Photographers (owners)** create an account with email or Google, then manage everything
  in **My Studio**: upload photos, edit photo details, create and order collections, choose the
  slides shown at the top of the profile, set a display name, bio, color and social links, change the
  profile link, and see visit statistics.

## How do I get to the main places?
- **Home page**: click the **Let me Lens** logo at top left of the home, sign-in and legal pages.
- **Create an account**: **Start the journey** or **Sign up** on the home page.
- **Sign in**: **Sign in** at the top right of the home page.
- **My Studio** (the owner area): open your gallery, click **Profile** at the top right, then
  **My Studio**. On a phone: tap the menu icon (three lines), then **Configuration**.
- **Inside My Studio** the left menu, titled **Configuration**, has four groups:
  **Photos** (New photo, Configure photos), **Collection** (New collection, Configure collection),
  **Profile** (Profile overview, About me, Social media, Colors, Carousel / Slides) and
  **Account** (Statistics, Account overview, Profile link, Password, Delete account).
- **Back to the public gallery** from My Studio: **View gallery** in the top bar.

## Key limits and rules
- Photo files: JPEG, PNG or WEBP, up to 15 MB each.
- Passwords: at least 8 characters (at most 100).
- Email verification code: 4 digits, valid 15 minutes.
- Password reset link: valid 30 minutes, usable once.
- Profile link: at least 3 and at most 80 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and dashes.
- Collections and photos are public as soon as they are saved; there is no private or draft mode.

## What Let Me Lens does not do (today)
- No comments, likes, followers or messaging between users.
- No contact form: visitors contact photographers through the email, LinkedIn or Instagram links the
  photographer chose to show.
- No choice of page layout; owners customise the gallery with one **Primary color**.
- Photo **Themes** are displayed under **More details**, but there is no button to set them yourself.

## How do I get help?
Write to **support@letmelens.com** (also used for privacy requests). The address is in the home page
footer and in the **Privacy Notice**.
