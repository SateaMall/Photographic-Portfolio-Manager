# Is Satea AlMallouhi a photographer? (Satea's photography and story)

## What is Satea AlMallouhi's story as a photographer?
Satea AlMallouhi is a photographer from **Homs, Syria**, a city deeply marked by war and by several years
of siege. In a context where documenting reality could become a risky act, Satea became interested in
the traces of the conflict and in the daily life that goes on despite everything, in a city that lost
half of its population. Satea left Syria in 2019 to seek asylum and to learn French in Paris, then
continued studies in Montpellier.

## What kind of photography does Satea AlMallouhi do?
Now settled in France, Satea AlMallouhi practises a sensitive photography, attentive to **ordinary
lives, silent spaces and invisible stories**. Satea's photographs can be seen on Satea's own Let Me Lens
gallery, `letmelens.com/satea-almallouhi`, which is also the example gallery of the website.

## Has Satea AlMallouhi won any photography prize?
Yes. Satea AlMallouhi won the **1st regional prize of the CROUS photo contest** (Concours Photo CROUS).
