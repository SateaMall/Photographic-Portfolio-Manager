# Why did Satea AlMallouhi create Let Me Lens, and what are Satea's skills and projects?

## Why did Satea AlMallouhi create Let Me Lens?
Satea AlMallouhi started Let Me Lens in 2025, after finishing studies, because Satea believes that
photographers should have **free access to a portfolio**. Photographers are artists who often do not earn
a good living from their work, and a free portfolio is a way to support them. Satea especially wants to
help **emerging photographers**, who may hesitate to show their work because of the cost of a portfolio
website: Let Me Lens is free, to encourage them to share what matters to them.

## How did Satea AlMallouhi build Let Me Lens?
Satea AlMallouhi designed and developed Let Me Lens alone, as an independent full-stack project, and it
runs in production. Satea built it in Java and TypeScript with REST APIs and a relational database
(Spring Boot, React, PostgreSQL, OpenAPI), automated its deployment on a Linux cloud server with Docker,
and set up a CI/CD pipeline with Jenkins. Satea also used the AI coding agent OpenCode to help with
tests, debugging and fixing security flaws.

According to Satea AlMallouhi's CV:
- **Languages and frameworks**: Java, Python, C#, JavaScript, TypeScript, Angular, React, Spring Boot,

## What other projects has Satea AlMallouhi built?
Besides Let Me Lens, Satea AlMallouhi's projects include:
- **Fake news analysis tool (LIRMM, CNRS, 2024)**: a web tool analysing fake news from 15 sources, with
  74,000 fact-checks extracted using SPARQL and a search engine (Python, Flask, Angular, Redis).
- **Inteco (University of Montpellier)**: an Android app (Java, Firebase) that connects job candidates
  with recruiters.
- **Hôtellerie (University of Montpellier)**: hotel booking services (Java Spring, REST APIs) queried by
  a travel agency that compares prices.
- **Machine learning and data warehouse projects (University of Montpellier)**: applying AI to data
  processing.
Several of these projects are on Satea's GitHub: github.com/SateaMall.
