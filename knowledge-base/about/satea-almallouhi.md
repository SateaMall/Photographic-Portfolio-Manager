# Who is Satea AlMallouhi, the creator of Let Me Lens?

## Who created Let Me Lens?
Let Me Lens was created by **Satea AlMallouhi** (full name Mohamad Satea AlMallouhi), written
"Satea ALMALLOUHI" in the footer of every page ("Created by Satea ALMALLOUHI"). Satea designed and
developed the whole website alone, since 2025: the photographers' galleries, the owner area
**My Studio**, the sign-in system, the server and its automated deployment.

## Who is Satea AlMallouhi in short?
Satea AlMallouhi is a full-stack software engineer and a photographer from Homs, Syria, now based in
Montpellier, France. Satea holds a Master's degree in Software Engineering (Data & AI) from the
University of Montpellier, worked as a software engineer at Ivalua and as a developer at the LIRMM
research laboratory (CNRS), and won the 1st regional prize of the CROUS photo contest. Satea created
Let Me Lens to give photographers, especially emerging ones, a free portfolio.

## Where can I see Satea AlMallouhi's photography?
Satea AlMallouhi's own gallery on Let Me Lens is `letmelens.com/satea-almallouhi`. It is also the
example gallery of the site: the **Exemple** link in the home page menu, the **Example** button at the
bottom of the home page and the **Satea ALMALLOUHI** link in the footers all open it.
