# How do I delete my Let Me Lens account?

## How do I permanently delete my account?
1. Open **My Studio** (**Profile → My Studio**, or **Configuration** on a phone).
2. In the left menu, under **Account**, click **Delete account**.
3. Click the red **Delete account** button.
4. A box asks you to type `DELETE` (in capital letters). Type it and confirm.
Your account is deleted, you are signed out and the home page opens. If you type anything else or
cancel, nothing is deleted.

## What is deleted with my account?
Deleting a Let Me Lens account is **permanent and cannot be undone**. It removes your gallery and
profile, all your photos and their stored files, all your collections, your slides, your settings and
your sign-in access. Your gallery link stops working. Download or keep copies of your original photos
before deleting.

## Can I delete my account without signing in?
No button on Let Me Lens deletes an account without signing in. If you cannot sign in, reset your
password with **Forgot password?**, or write to **support@letmelens.com** to ask for erasure of your
data.
