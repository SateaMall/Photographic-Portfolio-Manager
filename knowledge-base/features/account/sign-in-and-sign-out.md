# How do I sign in and sign out?

## How do I sign in to Let Me Lens with my email?
1. Click **Sign in** at the top right of the Let Me Lens home page. The **Welcome back** page opens.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.
Your own gallery opens. If you were trying to open a page of **My Studio**, Let Me Lens takes you back
to that page instead.

## How do I sign in with Google?
On the Let Me Lens **Welcome back** page, click **Continue with Google** and pick your Google account.
If you first created your account with email and password and already verified it, signing in with a
Google account that uses the same email links Google to that account, and both methods then work.

## How do I sign out of Let Me Lens?
- From your gallery: click **Profile** at the top right, then **Sign out**. On a phone: three-line
  menu icon, then **Sign out**.
- From My Studio: click **Sign out** in the top bar (on a phone, in the three-line menu).
You are taken back to the home page.

## Do I stay signed in?
Let Me Lens keeps you signed in with an essential session cookie until you click **Sign out** or the
session expires. Sign out on shared computers.
