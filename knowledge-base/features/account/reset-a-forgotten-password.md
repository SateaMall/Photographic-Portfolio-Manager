# I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

## How do I reset a forgotten Let Me Lens password?
1. On the **Welcome back** sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**. The **Forgot your password?** page
   opens (your email is pre-filled if you typed it).
2. Enter your **Email** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email "Reset your password" and click the link inside. The **Create a new password** page
   opens.
4. Enter a **New password** (at least 8 characters) and the same in **Confirm new password**.
5. Click **Update password**. You return to the sign-in page with "Password updated. You can sign in
   now.".

## How long is the password reset link valid?
A Let Me Lens password reset link is valid for **30 minutes** and can be used **only once**. If it has
expired, the page shows "Invalid or expired password reset link."; request a new one with
**Forgot password?**. You must wait 60 seconds between two requests and can ask for at most 6 links
per hour.

## Why did I not receive a reset email?
For privacy, Let Me Lens always answers "If an account exists for that email, a reset link has been
sent.", even when no account uses that address. No email is sent when the address has no account, or
when the account has not verified its email yet (finish **Verify your email** first). Also check the
spam folder and the spelling of the address.

## I signed up with Google. Do I need a password?
No. If you created your Let Me Lens account with **Continue with Google**, keep using
**Continue with Google** to sign in. If you also want an email password, sign in with Google, then set
one in **My Studio → Account → Password** (leave **Current password** empty).
