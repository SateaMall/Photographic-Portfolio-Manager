# How do I change my password?

## How do I change my Let Me Lens password while signed in?
1. Open **My Studio**: in your gallery click **Profile**, then **My Studio** (on a phone: three-line
   menu, then **Configuration**).
2. In the left menu, under **Account**, click **Password**.
3. Fill in **Current password**, **New password** (8 to 100 characters) and **Confirm new password**.
4. Click **Update password**. The message "Password updated." confirms the change.
If you do not remember your current password, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in
page instead.

## Can I add a password to an account created with Google?
Yes. If you signed up on Let Me Lens with **Continue with Google**, your account has no password yet.
Go to **My Studio → Account → Password**, leave **Current password** empty, type the **New password**
twice and click **Update password**. You can then sign in with Google or with your email and password.

## What errors can appear when changing the password?
On the Let Me Lens **Password** section, the messages mean:
- "New password must be at least 8 characters." – choose a longer password.
- "New password and confirmation must match." – retype the same password in both fields.
- "Current password is incorrect." – the current password is wrong.
- "Current password is required." – fill in **Current password**.
