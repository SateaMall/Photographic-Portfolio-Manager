# How do I change my gallery link (profile link)?

## How do I change my Let Me Lens gallery address?
Your gallery address is `letmelens.com/<profile-link>`. To change the part after the slash:
1. Open **My Studio** (**Profile → My Studio**, or **Configuration** on a phone).
2. In the left menu, under **Account**, click **Profile link**.
3. Type the new link in **Gallery link slug**. The line below shows a preview:
   "Your public link will be letmelens.com/your-new-link".
4. Click **Save link**. The message "Profile link updated." confirms it.

## What are the rules for a profile link?
A Let Me Lens profile link must be **3 to 80 characters** long and unique. Capitals become lowercase,
accents are removed, and spaces or symbols become dashes, so "Jean Dupont Photo" becomes
`jean-dupont-photo`. If the link is already used, the message "That profile link is already taken."
appears: try another one.

## What happens to my old link after I change it?
After you save a new profile link on Let Me Lens, the old address stops working and no longer opens your
gallery (visitors are sent to the home page). Update the link everywhere you shared it: social
networks, business cards, email signature.
