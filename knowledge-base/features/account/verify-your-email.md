# How do I verify my email address?

## How do I enter my verification code?
After an email sign-up, Let Me Lens sends a **4-digit verification code** to your email address (email
subject "Verify your email"). On the **Verify your email** page:
1. Check that **Email** shows your address.
2. Type the 4 digits in **Verification code**.
3. Click **Verify email**.
Your account is activated, you are signed in automatically, and your new gallery opens.

## How long is the verification code valid?
A Let Me Lens verification code is valid for **15 minutes** and works once. After that, request a new
one with **Resend code**.

## I did not receive the code. How do I get a new one?
On the Let Me Lens **Verify your email** page, make sure the **Email** field is filled in, then click
**Resend code**. The message "A fresh verification code was sent." confirms it. Check your spam folder.
Limits: you must wait **60 seconds** between two requests, and you can request at most **6 codes per
hour**; otherwise the page tells you how long to wait.

## How do I get back to the verification page later?
If you close the Let Me Lens verification page, simply try to sign in with your email and password: the
site sends you back to **Verify your email** with "Please verify your email to continue.". Click
**Resend code** if your previous code has expired.
