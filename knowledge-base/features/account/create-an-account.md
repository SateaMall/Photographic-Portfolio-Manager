# How do I create a Let Me Lens account?

## How do I sign up with my email address?
On Let Me Lens, an account gives you your own photography gallery. To sign up with an email address:
1. On the home page, click **Start the journey** (top banner) or **Sign up** (bottom of the page).
   The **Create account** page opens.
2. Enter your **Email** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters.
3. Click **Next**. (The button stays disabled until the email is filled in and the password has
   8 characters or more.)
4. Enter your **First name**, **Last name** and **Confirm password** (the same password again).
5. Click **Create account**. The **Verify your email** page opens and a 4-digit code is sent to your
   email address.
6. Enter the code to finish. See "How do I verify my email address?".
Click **Back** on the second step to correct your email or password.

## How do I sign up with Google?
On the Let Me Lens **Create account** page (or the sign-in page), click **Continue with Google** and
choose your Google account. Let Me Lens creates your account and gallery with your Google name and
signs you in directly, with no verification code, because Google already verified the email. The
Google account must have a verified email address.

## What happens after I create my account?
Right after sign-up and verification, Let Me Lens creates your public gallery at
`letmelens.com/<first-name>-<last-name>` (a number is added if that link is taken), with your full name
as the display name, and opens it. The gallery is empty until you upload photos in **My Studio**.
You can change the link later in **My Studio → Account → Profile link**, and the display name in
**My Studio → Profile → About me**.

## What information is required to sign up?
For an email sign-up, Let Me Lens requires an email address, a password, a first name and a last name.
Everything else (bio, public email, social links, photos) is optional. See the **Privacy Notice** link
under the form for how this data is used.
