# How do I upload photos?

## How do I upload photos to my Let Me Lens gallery?
1. Open **My Studio** (**Profile → My Studio**; on a phone **Configuration**).
2. In the left menu, under **Photos**, click **New photo**. The box "Queue new photos" appears.
3. Drag your files onto the box, or click **Choose files** and select one or several photos.
   They appear under **Pending uploads**.
4. Check or fill in the details of each photo (see below).
5. Click **Save photos**. The message "Saved 3 photos." (with your number) confirms the upload.
The photos appear immediately in the **Photos** grid of your public gallery.

## What file types and sizes are accepted?
Let Me Lens accepts **JPEG** (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`), **PNG** and **WEBP** files of up to **15 MB each**.
Other formats (HEIC, RAW, TIFF, GIF…) are skipped with the message "Skipped unsupported or duplicate
files: …", and larger files with "Skipped files over 15MB: …". Export HEIC or RAW photos to JPEG first.
Adding the same file twice to the queue is also skipped.

## Which details can I fill in before uploading?
Each photo in the Let Me Lens upload queue has these fields, all optional:
- **Title**: pre-filled from the file name (dashes and underscores become spaces).
- **Description**: shown under **More details** in the photo viewer.
- **Country**: type a country name or code (for example "France" or "FR") and pick it from the list.
- **City**: for example "Paris".
- **Year**: filled in automatically from the photo's camera data when available; must be between 1800
  and 2100.
You can change all of these later with **Edit photo**.

## How do I remove a photo from the queue before uploading?
In the Let Me Lens **Pending uploads** list, click the **×** on a photo to remove only that photo, or
**Clear queue** to remove all of them. Nothing is uploaded until you click **Save photos**.

## What happens if an upload fails?
When some uploads fail, Let Me Lens saves the others and keeps the failed photos in the queue, with the
message "Saved N photos. Failed uploads stayed in the queue." and the reason in red. Fix the problem and
click **Save photos** again. If you leave the photo screens of My Studio or close the page before
clicking **Save photos**, the queue is emptied and the files must be added again.

## Can I upload directly into a collection?
Not in one step. On Let Me Lens, upload the photos with **New photo** first, then open the collection in
**Configure collection** and click **Add to collection** on each photo in the **Photo library**. The
collection editor also has an **Add new photo** button that opens the upload queue.
