# How do I edit or delete one of my photos?

## How do I edit the title, description or place of a photo?
On Let Me Lens you edit a photo from the photo itself, while signed in:
1. Open the photo: click it in your gallery, or go to **My Studio → Photos → Configure photos** and
   click **Edit** on the photo.
2. Next to the photo, click **Edit photo**.
3. Change **Title**, **Description**, **Country**, **City** or **Capture year**.
4. Click **Save changes**. The message "Photo updated." confirms it. **Cancel** closes the form without
   saving.
The **Edit photo** and **Delete photo** buttons only appear on your own photos, when you are signed in.

## What format must the capture year have?
On Let Me Lens, **Capture year** must be a 4-digit year such as `2024`, between 1800 and 2100, or left
empty. Otherwise the message "Capture year must be a 4-digit year." appears.

## How do I delete a photo?
1. Open your photo on Let Me Lens (from your gallery, or **Configure photos → Edit**).
2. Click **Delete photo** (next to **Edit photo**, or at the bottom of the edit form).
3. Confirm the question "Delete this photo permanently?".
The photo is removed from your gallery for good, including from the collections and slides where it
appeared. This cannot be undone.

## Can I replace the image file of a photo?
No. On Let Me Lens the image file of an uploaded photo cannot be replaced. Upload the new version with
**New photo**, add it where needed, then delete the old photo.

## Can I set themes on my photos?
Not yet. The **Themes** shown under **More details** cannot be set from My Studio today. Title,
description, country, city and year are the details you can edit.
