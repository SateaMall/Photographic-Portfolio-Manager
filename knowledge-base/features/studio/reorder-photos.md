# How do I change the order of photos in my gallery?

## How do I reorder the photos of my Let Me Lens gallery?
The order of the **Photos** grid on your public gallery is set on the **Homepage photo order** screen:
1. Open **My Studio** and, under **Photos**, click **Configure photos**.
2. Grab a photo by its handle (the dotted grip icon) and drag it to its new place.
3. Release it. The order is saved automatically: "Homepage order saved automatically for N photos.".
Your public gallery shows the photos in this order. With a keyboard, focus the grip, press Space,
move with the arrow keys and press Space again to drop.

## Where do newly uploaded photos appear in the order?
On Let Me Lens, new photos that you have not placed yet appear after the photos you already ordered,
newest first. Open **Configure photos** and drag them where you want them.

## What if saving the order fails?
If Let Me Lens cannot save the new order, a red message appears with a **Retry save** button. Click
**Retry save**. If it keeps failing, reload the page and try again.

## Does this order also change my collections or slides?
No. On Let Me Lens, **Homepage photo order** only controls the main **Photos** grid. Each collection
has its own **Collection order** (in **Configure collection**), and the slides have their own
**Carousel order** (in **Carousel / Slides**).
