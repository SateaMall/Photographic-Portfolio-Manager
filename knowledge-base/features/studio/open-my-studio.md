# How do I open My Studio to manage my portfolio?

## How do I open My Studio on a computer?
**My Studio** is the private area of Let Me Lens where you manage your own gallery.
1. Sign in. Your gallery opens (or click **Open gallery** on the home page).
2. Click **Profile** at the top right of your gallery.
3. Click **My Studio**.
My Studio opens on **Profile overview**. If you are not signed in, Let Me Lens first shows the sign-in
page and then brings you to My Studio.

## How do I open My Studio on a phone?
On a phone, the Let Me Lens gallery menu is hidden behind the menu icon (three lines) at the top
right. Tap it, then tap **Configuration**. It opens the same My Studio as on a computer. Inside
My Studio, tap the three-line icon in the top bar to see the **Configuration** menu.

## How is My Studio organised?
My Studio on Let Me Lens has a top bar and a left menu.
- The top bar shows **Manage** with your display name (click it to return to the start of My Studio),
  **View gallery** (opens your public gallery) and **Sign out**.
- The left menu, titled **Configuration**, has four groups: **Photos** (**New photo**,
  **Configure photos**), **Collection** (**New collection**, **Configure collection**), **Profile**
  (**Profile overview**, **About me**, **Social media**, **Colors**, **Carousel / Slides**) and
  **Account** (**Statistics**, **Account overview**, **Profile link**, **Password**,
  **Delete account**).
- The arrow button at the top of the left menu collapses it to give more room, and expands it again.

## Can I manage someone else's gallery?
No. Each Let Me Lens account manages only its own gallery. **My Studio** always opens your own
gallery's settings, and pages of another gallery show "This page is only available for your own main
profile.".

## What is the fastest way to set up a new portfolio?
A good order on Let Me Lens is:
1. **New photo**: upload your photos and fill in titles and places.
2. **New collection**: group photos into collections and order them.
3. **Carousel / Slides**: choose the slides shown at the top of your gallery.
4. **About me**, **Social media**, **Colors**: write a bio, add contact links, pick a color, then
   click **Save profile**.
5. **Profile link**: choose a short link and share it.
6. **View gallery** to check the result.
