# How do I create a collection (album)?

## How do I create a new collection on Let Me Lens?
1. Open **My Studio** (**Profile → My Studio**; on a phone **Configuration**).
2. In the left menu, under **Collection**, click **New collection**. The **New collection** form appears
   at the top left.
3. Type a **Title** (required) and, if you want, a **Description**.
4. Click **Create album**.
The collection is created and its editor opens on the right, ready for you to add photos from the
**Photo library**. See "How do I add or remove photos in a collection?".

## Why use collections?
Collections let a Let Me Lens photographer present work as series: a trip, a wedding, a project or a
theme. Each collection gets its own page with a title, a description and its own photo order, appears
as a card in the **Collections** row of the gallery, and has its own link that can be shared on its own.
A photo can belong to several collections without being uploaded twice.

## Is a new collection public right away?
Yes. On Let Me Lens a collection appears in the **Collections** row of your public gallery as soon as it
is created, even before it has photos (it then shows "No cover" and "0 photos"). There is no private
or draft mode, so add photos right after creating it, or delete it with **Delete album**.

## What if I forget the title?
If the **Title** is empty, Let Me Lens shows "Album title is required." and does not create the
collection.
