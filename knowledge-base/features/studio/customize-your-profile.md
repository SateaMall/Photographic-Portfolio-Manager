# How do I change my name, bio, color and social links?

## Where do I edit my profile on Let Me Lens?
All profile details are on one page of My Studio, "Profile configuration" ("Tune the identity of your
gallery."). Open **My Studio** and click **Profile overview** under **Profile**. The links **About me**,
**Social media** and **Colors** jump to each part of the same page. After any change, click
**Save profile** at the bottom: the message "Profile settings saved." confirms it. Nothing is saved
until you click **Save profile**.

## How do I change my display name or bio?
In the **About me** part of the Let Me Lens profile page:
1. Change **Display name**: the name shown on your gallery, your bio card and in page titles. It cannot
   be empty ("Display name is required.").
2. Write your **Bio**: a short text shown under your name at the top of your gallery.
3. Click **Save profile**.

## How do I change the color of my gallery?
In the **Colors** part of the Let Me Lens profile page, set **Primary color**: click the color square
to pick a color, or type a hex code such as `#fcfcfc` in the field next to it. Then click
**Save profile**. A code that is not a valid hex color shows "Primary color must be a valid hex
value.". The color is applied to your public gallery and photo viewer.

## How do I add my email, LinkedIn or Instagram?
In the **Social media** part of the Let Me Lens profile page ("Add public links and contact details
visible to visitors"):
- **Public email**: the address visitors can copy with the envelope icon on your gallery. It can differ
  from your sign-in email.
- **LinkedIn username**: only the end of your LinkedIn address, for example `jean-dupont` for
  `linkedin.com/in/jean-dupont`.
- **Instagram username**: your handle, with or without `@`, for example `jeandupont`.
Click **Save profile**. Each filled field adds an icon on your gallery; empty the field to remove it.
