# How do I see how many people visit my gallery?

## Where are the visit statistics on Let Me Lens?
Open **My Studio** and, under **Account**, click **Statistics**. The **Portfolio statistics** page
shows three numbers:
- **All time**: every visit since your gallery started being tracked.
- **Last 30 days**: visits over the last 30 days, today included.
- **Today**: visits since midnight (UTC).

## How do I read the visits chart?
Under the totals, the Let Me Lens **Visits timeline** shows a bar per month. Click **Monthly** or
**Yearly** to switch between monthly and yearly totals. Hover a bar to see the exact number of visits.
Drag the handles of the zoom bar under the chart to focus on a period. "No portfolio visits have been
recorded in this period yet." means there were no visits.

## What counts as a visit?
On Let Me Lens a visit is counted each time someone opens your public gallery (or one of its
collections or photos) in their browser. Your own visits while signed in are **not** counted. Days are
counted in UTC time.
