# How do I choose the slides at the top of my gallery?

## How do I choose the slides shown at the top of my Let Me Lens gallery?
The slides are the large photos that scroll at the top of your gallery. To choose them:
1. Open **My Studio** and, under **Profile**, click **Carousel / Slides**. The page "Manage Slides"
   opens.
2. In **Photo library**, click **Add to carousel** on each photo you want as a slide. It moves up to
   **Carousel order**.
3. In **Carousel order**, drag the slides by their grip handle to set their order.
4. Click **Remove** on a slide to take it out of the carousel (the photo stays in your gallery).
Every change saves automatically ("Carousel changes saved automatically."). Click **View profile** to
see the result.

## What is shown if I do not choose any slides?
If the carousel of a Let Me Lens gallery is empty, the gallery shows the first photos of your **Photos**
grid (up to 5) as slides instead. When you remove every slide, the message "Carousel cleared. The
profile page will fall back to the first photos in your grid." confirms it.

## Why choose slides carefully?
On Let Me Lens the slides are the first thing visitors see, full width at the top of your gallery, and
the first slide is also used as the preview image when your gallery link is shared. Pick a few strong,
wide photos that represent your style. Slides change automatically every 7 seconds.
