# How do I manage a collection (add photos, reorder, rename, delete)?

## How do I open a collection to edit it?
1. Open **My Studio** and, under **Collection**, click **Configure collection**. The page
   "Manage your collections" opens.
2. On the left, the **Collections** list shows each collection with its cover and number of photos.
   Click a collection to open its editor on the right.
The editor has three parts: **Collection details**, **Collection order** and **Photo library**.

## How do I add or remove photos in a collection?
In the Let Me Lens collection editor, the **Photo library** at the bottom lists all your photos.
- Click **Add to collection** on a photo to add it. The button then shows **Added**.
- Click **Added** again, or **Remove** on the photo in **Collection order**, to take it out of the
  collection. The photo itself stays in your gallery.
Every change is saved automatically: "Collection changes saved automatically. N photos currently in
the collection.". To add photos you have not uploaded yet, click **Add new photo**.

## How do I reorder the photos inside a collection?
In **Collection order** of the Let Me Lens collection editor, drag a photo by its grip handle to a new
place and release it. The order is saved automatically and is the order visitors see on the
collection page.

## How do I choose the cover of a collection?
On Let Me Lens the cover of a collection is always its **first photo** in **Collection order**. Drag the
photo you want as cover to the first place.

## How do I rename a collection or change its description?
In **Collection details** of the Let Me Lens collection editor, change the **Title** or
**Description**, then click **Save details**. The message "Collection details saved." confirms it. The
title cannot be empty.

## How do I change the order of my collections?
In the **Collections** list on the left of the Let Me Lens collection page, drag a collection by its
grip handle to a new place. The new order is saved automatically ("Saving collection order...") and is
the order of the **Collections** row on your gallery.

## How do I delete a collection?
In **Collection details**, click **Delete album** and confirm "Delete album "…" permanently?". On Let Me
Lens this deletes only the collection: the photos it contained stay in your gallery and in your other
collections.

## How do I see the collection as visitors see it?
Click **View collection** at the bottom of the Let Me Lens collection editor. The public collection page
opens.
