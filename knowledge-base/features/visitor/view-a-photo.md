# How do I view a photo in full screen and see its details?

## How do I open a photo on Let Me Lens?
Click any photo in a Let Me Lens gallery or collection grid. The photo opens in a viewer window over the
page, with its title and details next to it. Close the viewer with the **✕** at the top, or with your
browser's back button.

## How do I see a photo in full screen or zoom in?
In the Let Me Lens photo viewer, click the photo itself (the hint says "Click to fullscreen / zoom").
The photo opens full screen, where you can zoom in. Click outside the photo, or on the photo, to leave
full screen.

## What details are shown for a photo?
Next to the photo, Let Me Lens shows the photo title ("Untitled" if the photographer gave none),
followed by the city and country, the year the photo was taken and the photographer's name, when
known. Click **More details** to see the photo's description and its **Themes**
(for example "Street & Society"); "—" means no theme is set.

## What are the Related photos under a photo?
When a photo is opened from the main **Photos** grid, Let Me Lens shows **Related photos** under it:
other photos by the same photographer, favouring the same themes, country and city. Click one to open
it. Photos opened from a collection show the collection's **‹** / **›** arrows instead.

## Can I share a link to one photo?
Yes. On Let Me Lens every photo has its own address, shown in the browser's address bar when the photo
is open (it looks like `letmelens.com/<profile-link>/photo/...`). Copy that address and send it. The
person opening it sees the photo on its own page, with a link back to the photographer's gallery.

## Can I download photos from Let Me Lens?
Let Me Lens has no download button. The photos belong to their photographer; to use a photo, contact
the photographer through the links on their gallery.
