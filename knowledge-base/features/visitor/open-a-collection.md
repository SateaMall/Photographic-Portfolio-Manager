# How do I open a collection (album)?

## How do I open a collection on Let Me Lens?
On a Let Me Lens gallery, collections are listed in the **Collections** row under the photographer's
bio card.
1. Click **Collections** in the top menu, or scroll down to the **Collections** row.
2. Use the **‹** and **›** arrows (or swipe) to find the collection.
3. Click the collection card (cover, title and number of photos). The collection page opens.
If the photographer has no collection, the **Collections** row is not shown.

## What is on a collection page?
A Let Me Lens collection page shows, from top to bottom: the top menu with the photographer's name
(click it to go back to the gallery), slides made of the first photos of the collection, the
collection title with "collection by <photographer>" and its description, then the grid
**Collection's photos** with every photo of the collection in the order chosen by the photographer.

## How do I move from one photo to the next in a collection?
Open a photo from a Let Me Lens collection page. In the photo viewer, the **‹** (Previous photo) and
**›** (Next photo) arrows on each side of the photo move through the collection in order. These arrows
only appear for photos opened from a collection.
