# How do I browse a photographer's gallery?

## What is on a Let Me Lens gallery page?
A Let Me Lens gallery page (`letmelens.com/<profile-link>`) belongs to one photographer and can be
opened by anyone, without an account. From top to bottom it shows:
1. The top menu with **Collections**, **Photos** and **Contact** (on a phone: the three-line menu icon).
2. The **slides**: large photos that change every 7 seconds, with **‹** and **›** arrows.
3. A card with the photographer's display name, bio and contact icons.
4. **Collections**: a row of collection cards, each with a cover photo, a title and the number of
   photos. Use the **‹** and **›** arrows or swipe to scroll the row.
5. **Photos**: the photographer's photos in a grid.
6. The footer "Created with © 2026 Let Me Lens by Satea ALMALLOUHI".
If the address does not match any gallery, Let Me Lens sends you back to the home page.

## How do I see more photos in a gallery?
A Let Me Lens gallery first shows about 20 photos in the **Photos** grid with a fade at the bottom.
1. Click **See more** under the grid to reveal the rest of the loaded photos.
2. If the photographer has more photos, click **Load more photos** to load the next batch. Repeat
   until the button disappears.
Click any photo to open it in the photo viewer.

## What do Collections, Photos and Contact do in the gallery menu?
In the top menu of a Let Me Lens gallery, **Collections** scrolls to the row of collections,
**Photos** scrolls to the photo grid, and **Contact** scrolls to the card with the photographer's
contact icons. The down arrow at the bottom of the slides also scrolls to the content.

## How do I contact a photographer on Let Me Lens?
Let Me Lens has no messaging or contact form. Each photographer decides which contact links to show on
the card under the slides:
- The **LinkedIn** icon opens the photographer's LinkedIn profile in a new tab.
- The **Instagram** icon opens the photographer's Instagram account in a new tab.
- The **Email** icon (envelope) copies the photographer's public email address to your clipboard and
  shows "Email copied!". Paste it into your email app.
If no icon is shown, the photographer has not published any contact details.

## Why do I see a Profile button on someone else's gallery?
The **Profile** button at the top right of a gallery only appears when you are signed in to your own
Let Me Lens account. Its menu (**My Studio**, **Sign out**) always refers to your own account, even
when you are looking at another photographer's gallery.
