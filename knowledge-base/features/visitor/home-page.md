# What can I do on the Let Me Lens home page?

## What is on the Let Me Lens home page?
The Let Me Lens home page (`letmelens.com`) presents the service to photographers. From top to bottom
it shows:
1. A large banner: "Let us help you share what matters to you." and "Prepare your photography
   portfolio in 5 minutes.", with the button **Start the journey**.
2. Four sections that appear while scrolling: **Elegant & Engaging**, **Cross-Device Experience**,
   **Customizable** and **Analytics**, each with a preview image.
3. A final section "Ready to share your work?" with the buttons **Sign up** and **Example**.
4. The footer: "© 2026 Let Me Lens", "Created by Satea ALMALLOUHI" (a link to Satea's gallery), the
   support address **support@letmelens.com** and the **Privacy Notice** link.

## What do the buttons in the home page menu do?
The menu at the top of the Let Me Lens home page has:
- **Let me Lens** (logo, left): back to the home page.
- **Exemple**: opens the example gallery of Satea AlMallouhi, the creator of the site.
- **Why Us**: scrolls down to the sections explaining the advantages.
- **Sign in**: opens the sign-in page. When you are already signed in, this button is called
  **Open gallery** and opens your own gallery.
On a phone, these links are behind the menu icon (three lines) at the top right.

## What does the Start the journey button do?
On the Let Me Lens home page, **Start the journey** opens the **Create account** page when you are not
signed in. When you are signed in, the same button is called **Open gallery** and takes you to your own
gallery.
